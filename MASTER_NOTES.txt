Frontend baseline: existing premium UI direction preserved; no intentional redesign.

Current authentication:
- Firebase Phone Authentication sends and verifies the driver SMS OTP.

- After successful OTP, the app calls the secure Firebase callable ensureDriverAccount function.
- The backend assigns the driver custom claim and creates/updates partners/{uid}.
- The client force-refreshes the Firebase ID token so Firestore/Storage rules immediately recognize role=driver.
